sells, who its customers are, and what those customers care about**. All facts
below were verified against the official website (omniaccess.com). Any agent
that authors content about OmniAccess, its products, or its customers should
read this file in full before writing.

**Usage rule — framing vs. facts.** This file provides *company background*
and *framing*. Product-specific facts, specs, procedures and capabilities in
any output must come from the **source extract**. Company-level facts from
this file (fleet size, SOC, segments, portfolio names) MAY be used to set

## 1. What OmniAccess is

- Founded **2002 in Palma de Mallorca** (founders Rita & Bertrand, originally
  an IT support team for superyachts); part of the **Marlink Group since 2018**
  (sister brands: Marlink, Telemar, Marlink Cyber).
- The leading provider of **premium digital solutions for the maritime
  industry**: connectivity, network, cloud & IT, IoT & applications,
  cybersecurity, unified by the UNITY platform.
- Tagline: *"Delivering Possibilities Anywhere"*. Vision: *"Pushing boundaries
  to reach the unreachable."*
- Operating model: **fully managed service**, not a box-seller — 24/7
  **Technical Support Center (TSC)** (formerly called the NOC / Network
  Operations Centre — "TSC" is the current term; use it, and treat any "NOC"
  reference as the same thing), fully owned teleports, and a fully certified
  24/7 Security Operations Center (SOC). These (TSC, SOC, teleports) are
  **OmniAccess's own** service functions — never the customer's internal teams.

## 2. Solution portfolio

Every specific product in training material (captive portal, Unity Local
Admin, Ucopia, Fusion plans, …) hangs off one of these areas. Content should
anchor the product into this map so the reader can place it in the bigger
picture.

**Connectivity — nextGen Fusion / Fusion 2.0.** Multi-orbit managed
connectivity blending **Starlink (LEO), Eutelsat OneWeb (LEO), O3b mPower,
GEO VSAT and 4G/5G** into one seamless service. Highlights: **one contract,
one data plan, all carriers** (a single vessel-specific data quota consumed
across all networks); carrier-agnostic by design (blend and select networks
by performance/availability/regulatory coverage); plans from 500GB to 25TB
plus PAYG, monthly up/downgrades; usage visible in the customer portal;
built-in NGFW security. How OmniAccess describes it: *"Consistent,
high-performance connectivity wherever the journey takes you."*

**Network — NaaS (Network as a Service).** Smart onboard networks, designed,
monitored and managed 24/7. Offerings: NaaS 24/7 Support (take over existing
infrastructure), NaaS Turnkey (design + deploy + operate), WiFi Planning
Survey, Network Audit (e.g. LEO-readiness for Starlink/OneWeb), consultancy,
bonding, **Captive Portal** (guest access management: custom login pages,
authentication, user control), Network Access Control, Private MPLS.
Certified team (Cisco CCNP, Fortinet NSE, Aruba, ITIL, PMP). How OmniAccess
describes it: *"Proactive monitoring catches incidents before your guests
notice."*

**Cloud & IT.** Hybrid cloud integration, cloud migration, backup strategies,
secure data management/compliance, cost optimization with end-to-end SLAs.
How OmniAccess describes it: *"Resiliency and productivity for onboard
operations — we handle the complexity."*

**IoT & Applications.** Remote vessel management, integrated guest experience
(environment, entertainment, concierge), real-time monitoring/alerts, ML/AI
for fleet performance. How OmniAccess describes it: *"A digitally intelligent
vessel — personalized experience for guests, efficiency for operations."*

**Cybersecurity.** Maritime-specialised, aligned with the **NIST 2.0
framework**; compliance drivers include **NIS2**/GDPR/ISO 27001. Full
lifecycle: Identify (attack-surface management, assessments, pentesting),
Protect (PAM, UTM, phishing & awareness training, DLP), Detect (24/7 SOC,
NDR, EDR, threat hunting, honeypots), Respond (MDR, forensics), Recover,
Govern (risk management, CISO-level strategy). **Tiered plans: Threat
Detection, Threat Detection+, CyberSec, CyberSec+, CISO as a Service.**
Technology partners: CrowdStrike, Fortinet, Palo Alto, Cisco, Splunk,
Microsoft, Tenable, Avanan. How OmniAccess describes it: *"Superyachts are
high-value targets; our certified 24/7 SOC safeguards your data, privacy and
operations."*

**UNITY — "The Possibility Platform".** OmniAccess's own platform, at the
heart of everything: one foundation that enables all onboard digital
solutions through a single intuitive interface ("single pane of glass").
Features: **bonding & Choose-my-IP** (pick your online country location),
integrated **nextGen firewall** + security log collector, **SD-WAN & traffic
shaping** (per-app/per-user prioritization and limits), **access control /
user management portal** (data-consumption and network-access policies in a
few clicks), modular design, hosts onboard apps, bridges to cloud, aggregates
IoT data. Designed in-house specifically for maritime — *"when you design a
platform from the ground up for a specific market, you achieve something far
superior to off-the-shelf appliances."*

## 3. Customer segments and what each one cares about

Pitch pillars OmniAccess uses across all segments: **24/7 Cybersecurity
Shield (SOC)**, **Premium Digital Experience**, **24/7 Premium Support (TSC)**.

**Superyacht (core segment, ~600 vessels).** *"Your Dreams, our Destination."*
The customer serves an owner and their guests. Value drivers: flawless
guest/owner experience (streaming, video calls, sharing from remote
anchorages), absolute **privacy and discretion**, crew welfare/retention, and
zero-hassle operation — the ETO/captain must never be embarrassed in front of
the owner. Price matters less than reliability and service. Includes the
**new-build** sub-segment (shipyards, project managers).

**Boutique cruise (~100+ vessels).** *"Set sail for unforgettable moments."*
A commercial operator. Value drivers: **guest satisfaction** (connectivity is
a top review factor), crew empowerment/welfare, fleet-wide operational
efficiency, predictable OPEX, compliance/security. More structured,
ROI-driven decisions than superyacht.

**Research vessel / USV.** *"Navigate beyond limits."* Mission-driven. Value
drivers: resilient communications in remote deployments, secure data
transfer/integration, remote operation (USV/ROV), efficiency.

## 4. Who decides and who influences (varies by client)

There is no single buyer persona; decision-making depends on the client's
size and type. Typical patterns:

- **Large fleets / cruise lines** → **IT Fleet Manager / IT Director**
  decides; structured procurement; cares about fleet-wide consistency, OPEX,
  SLAs, security posture.
- **Smaller vessels / smaller cruise operators** → the **Captain** often
  decides; cares about simplicity, reliability, no complaints from guests or
  owner.
- **Mid-size vessels / superyachts** → **Owner's Representative** or **Chief
  Engineer** drives the decision; balances owner experience with budget and
  technical fit.
- **Day-to-day technical influencer everywhere** → **ETO / IT / AV Officer**:
  lives with the product daily; values ease of use, visibility and support,
  and vetoes whatever makes their life harder.
- **Indirect channel** → yacht management companies and **shipyards (new
  builds)** influence or aggregate many decisions.

(OmniAccess's own contact forms list these roles: Captain, Vessel/Fleet
Manager, Chief Engineer, ETO/IT/AV Officer/Manager, Technical Manager,
Project Manager, Owner's Representative, Operations Manager.)
